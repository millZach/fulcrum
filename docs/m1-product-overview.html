06 / THE BOUNDARY

M1 finishes the creative front half.

It produces trustworthy visual ground truth. It does not quietly expand into asset optimization, environment construction, or gameplay.

After M1, Fulcrum can…

  • Interrogate a varied brief until it is genuinely understood
  • Maintain the game's glossary and important design decisions
  • Offer and redirect multiple visual directions
  • Generate a demand-driven concept set
  • Regenerate one concept without disturbing its siblings
  • Show exact creative lineage and inherited visual tokens

It does not yet…

  • Improve mesh topology, textures, or 3D-provider strategy
  • Generate multiview concepts or modular environment kits
  • Refine assets in Blender
  • Build gameplay or a playable vertical slice
  • Run autonomous visual-improvement loops or Gauntlets
  • Require another paid image-to-3D acceptance run
