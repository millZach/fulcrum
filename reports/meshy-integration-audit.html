Fulcrum · Meshy audit · 25 August 2026

Meshy rebuilt what Fulcrum showed it.

No prompt was sent to Meshy. M2 sent four generated reference images per hero. The weak link was reference preparation, followed by wasteful 30-credit all-at-once generation.

What was sent

Four PNG references ordered front, left, back, right through Multi-Image to 3D using Meshy 6.

What was not sent

No Meshy text prompt, no bridge acceptance criteria, no texture-specific references, and no geometry-only approval stage.

Keeper · exact Meshy inputs

Coherent shape, noisy surface direction.

The four views describe the same character well. They also ask for dense patina, cloth fibers, tiny dark mechanisms, and low value separation—then Fulcrum immediately bakes a 2K texture before QA.

Bridge · original exact inputs

The bridge was already missing.

Three images show hoists. The back image shows a different circular optical mechanism. None has a traversable span, approach, or keeper-scale bridge controls.

Root cause

The asset plan contained the right bridge criteria, but the concept-view compiler accepts only the asset name and global visual tokens. It spent 3,995 characters on world lore and never told the image model it needed a deck.

Bridge · regenerated inputs

More consistent. Still not a bridge.

Fulcrum kept the original front and replaced three views. QA's critical missing-deck finding lost priority to a major side-silhouette finding. The selected correction was then truncated from the 4,000-character image prompt.
